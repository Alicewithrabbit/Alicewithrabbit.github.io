Dr. Chong Wu 吳崇

AI Researcher at Leibniz

I explore new directions in foundation model theory and work to refine the principles and architectures behind existing models. My research on AI infrastructure focuses on which architectural choices deliver high performance under real-world serving workloads.

I received my PhD from the City University of Hong Kong, where I worked with Prof. Hong Yan on superpixel models and graph neural networks for visual computing.
